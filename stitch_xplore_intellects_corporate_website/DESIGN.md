## Brand & Style

This design system embodies a modern, minimal, and premium corporate technology ethos tailored for high-stakes enterprise consulting, intellectual capital, and digital transformation. It projects composure, technical authority, and precision.

### Personality & Tone
- **Authoritative yet Approachable:** Built on structural discipline, deep navy tones, and decisive typography, avoiding tech gimmickry.
- **Architectural Clarity:** Generous whitespace, razor-sharp alignment, and intentional hierarchy prioritize rapid cognitive processing for enterprise stakeholders and C-suite decision-makers.
- **Understated Precision:** Rather than relying on heavy gradients or loud animations, tactile quality comes from meticulously calibrated borders, refined micro-elevation, and typographic contrast.

### Aesthetic Foundation
- **Modern Corporate Minimalism:** High-contrast information hierarchy built on clean geometry, deliberate surface tiers, and quiet functional accents.
- **Avoidance of Clutter:** Explicitly excludes noisy glassmorphism, saturated multi-stop gradients, and decorative noise in favor of pure surfaces, crisp division lines, and rhythmic 8px spacing.

## Colors

The color palette reinforces institutional trust, computational clarity, and executive maturity through deep architectural navies and vibrant trust-grade blues.

### Hierarchy & Application
- **Primary (`#1D4ED8`):** The operational core. Used for primary interactive triggers, active navigation items, progress indications, and key focal points.
- **Secondary (`#0B132B`):** Deep Navy. Forms high-authority surfaces, executive headers, contextual contrast bars, and deep-mode framing.
- **Tertiary (`#1C2541`):** Mid Navy. Serves as structural counterpoints, secondary card backings, and dark-theme elevated containers.
- **Neutral (`#0F172A`):** Dark Slate. Applied to core typography and primary iconography to achieve high readability without the harshness of pure black.
- **Surfaces (`#FFFFFF`, `#F8FAFC`, `#F1F5F9`):** Layered background tiers that establish depth without relying on complex color washes.
- **Borders (`#E2E8F0`, `#CBD5E1`):** Structural hairlines providing physical boundaries for cards, tables, and compartmentalized metrics.

## Typography

The typographic pairing balances modern architectural geometry with rigorous corporate legibility.

- **Headlines (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Introduces contemporary character, sharp geometric finishes, and executive energy. Display titles employ tight negative letter-spacing (`-0.02em` to `-0.025em`) to preserve density in large viewport formats.
- **Body & Functional UI (Inter):** Neutral, utilitarian, and optimized for complex enterprise data, tables, dashboards, and narrative case studies.
- **Optical Consistency:** Ensure headlines downscale reliably via mobile-specific tokens to prevent awkward hyphenation or wrapping in narrow mobile viewports.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a rigorous 8px mathematical grid coupled with intentional whitespace to maintain executive breathing room and structural balance.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column layout with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and a minimum outer margin of 48px (`3rem`). Standard container maximum width is clamped to 1280px for standard views and 1440px for analytical dashboards.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with 20px gutters and 32px outer margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 20px (`1.25rem`) outer canvas margins.

### Vertical Rhythm & Density
- **Micro Spacing (`space-xs`, `space-sm`):** Reserved for icon-to-label alignment, chip internals, and stacked inline values.
- **Component Padding (`space-md`, `space-lg`):** Standard container insets (`16px` to `24px`) ensuring cards, input fields, and panels feel structured and uncrowded.
- **Sectional Gaps (`space-xl` and multipliers):** Large macro distances (40px, 64px, 96px) separate thematic content blocks, establishing visual clarity without arbitrary separating lines.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses low-contrast physical outlines combined with multi-stop, ultra-diffused drop shadows. This avoids muddy grays and maintains a sharp, professional tone.

### The Elevation Stack
- **Level 0 (Flat/Base):** Canvas `#FFFFFF` or subtle panel `#F8FAFC`. Zero elevation, bounded strictly by structural 1px border `#E2E8F0`.
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Blocks):**
  - Border: `1px solid #E2E8F0`
  - Shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 4px 12px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`
- **Level 2 (Hover State & Interactive Elements):**
  - Border: `1px solid #CBD5E1`
  - Shadow: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 10px 24px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`
- **Level 3 (Dropdowns, Menus & Overlays):**
  - Border: `1px solid #E2E8F0`
  - Shadow: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 20px 32px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`
- **Level 4 (Modals & Executive Dialogs):**
  - Border: `1px solid #CBD5E1`
  - Shadow: `0 25px 50px -12px rgba(11, 19, 43, 0.22)`

All elevations must feature slate-tinted ambient attenuation rather than raw black alpha masks, reinforcing the cool, deliberate Navy palette.

## Shapes

The design system standardizes on calibrated rounded geometry to bridge technical discipline with modern corporate elegance.

### Geometry Standards
- **Core Radius (`roundedness: 2` / 0.5rem - 8px):** Applied to buttons, input fields, dropdown triggers, and interactive components.
- **Large Radius (`rounded-lg` / 0.75rem - 12px):** Default for cards, modular grid units, and small panels.
- **Extra Large Radius (`rounded-xl` / 1rem - 16px):** Standard for primary content containers, hero media frames, and modal dialogues.
- **Pill Variant:** Reserved strictly for status badges, numeric counters, and micro-tags. Never used for primary buttons or inputs.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled `#1D4ED8` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `font-weight: 500`. Height: 44px (default), 36px (compact). Padding: 12px 20px. Radius: 8px. Hover shifts to `#1E40AF` with a subtle Level 1 shadow. Focus introduces a 2px offset ring in `#1D4ED8`.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, text `#0F172A`. Hover transitions background to `#F8FAFC` and border to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** No border or surface. Text `#334155`. Hover transitions to `#F1F5F9` background and `#0F172A` text.

### Cards & Panels
- Constructed with `#FFFFFF` or `#F8FAFC` fill, 12px or 16px border-radius, and standard `1px solid #E2E8F0` framing.
- Interactive cards elevate from Level 1 to Level 2 on hover with a `transform: translateY(-2px)` transition across 200ms ease. Padding defaults to 24px (`space-lg`) on desktop and 16px (`space-md`) on mobile.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Height: 44px. Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, radius 8px. Font: Inter 14px. Placeholder text `#94A3B8`. Active/Focus state uses `border-color: #1D4ED8` with a 3px ring in `rgba(29, 78, 216, 0.12)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 18px box dimensions, radius 4px (checkbox) or circular (radio). Unchecked: `1px solid #CBD5E1` on `#FFFFFF`. Checked: Solid `#1D4ED8` fill with crisp `#FFFFFF` checkmark/dot icon.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Badges:** Pill-shaped (fully rounded), 24px height, horizontal padding 10px. Uses muted tinted backgrounds with saturated text:
  - Active/Success: Background `#F0FDF4`, Text `#15803D`
  - In-Progress/Info: Background `#EFF6FF`, Text `#1D4ED8`
  - Neutral/Draft: Background `#F1F5F9`, Text `#475569`
- **Filter Chips:** 8px radius, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, background `#FFFFFF`. Selected state swaps border and fill to `#0B132B` with `#FFFFFF` text.

### Lists & Data Displays
- Divided using clean `1px solid #F1F5F9` rules.
- Table headers set in `label-sm` with `#64748B` text, all-caps or title case, resting on `#F8FAFC` background. Table rows highlight with `#F8FAFC` on hover.
