# Xplore Intellects – Business Website UI/UX

A modern and responsive business website designed for Xplore Intellects as part of a UI/UX Technical Assessment.

## Project Overview

Xplore Intellects is a digital solutions company that focuses on helping businesses create measurable value through technology.

The website presents the company's digital services, business story, mission, vision, leadership, and consultation contact options in a clean and professional interface.

## Objective

The objective of this project is to design a professional, user-friendly, and responsive website that communicates Xplore Intellects' business-first approach.

## Key Features

- Modern and professional homepage design
- Responsive desktop and mobile layouts
- Sticky navigation bar
- Hero section with clear call-to-action buttons
- Company Story section
- Mission and Vision section
- Services section with eight digital services
- Leadership Team section
- Consultation contact form
- Structured footer with navigation and contact details

## Services Included

- Web Development
- Web Applications
- SEO
- Branding
- Mobile App Development
- Custom Business Platforms
- Digital Marketing
- Business Consulting

## Design Approach

The design follows a premium, minimal, and business-focused visual style.

### Design Principles

- Clean visual hierarchy
- Consistent spacing and alignment
- Easy navigation
- Responsive design
- Professional typography
- Accessible color contrast
- Clear call-to-action buttons
